Assumptions. Growth compounds annually. Scenario B injections are added at each year-end. In drawdown, each year's inflated expense is withdrawn, then the remaining balance grows at the post-exit rate; balance is floored at zero once depleted. Figures are nominal SGD and ignore tax, CPF, and market volatility. This is a planning aid, not financial advice.
